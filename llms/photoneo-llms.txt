# Photoneo

> Photoneo is a Slovak machine-vision company (Bratislava, founded 2013) and a global pioneer of high-resolution structured-light 3D measurement. Its PhoXi 3D Scanner family and MotionCam-3D cameras, paired with AI software (Bin Picking Studio, Locator, Palletizing), power robotic automation. Photoneo exposes a developer surface through the PhoXi Control software with official C++, C#, and Python device APIs, GenICam/GigE Vision connectivity, and TCP/IP robot interfaces — not a hosted web/REST API. Now part of the Brightpick Group; acquired by Zebra Technologies.
